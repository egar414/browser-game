# Asset provenance

The 225 Tender hull is adapted from the project owner's uploaded
`single_color_261030100313_WUGV.stl` (30 October 2026). It is decimated and fitted
to the game's crew and waterline, with the existing original 25 hp outboard.
Its source hash, mesh budgets and conversion procedure are recorded in
`docs/uploaded-225-tender.md` and `src/assets/tender225-mesh.json`.

Other watercraft, riders, procedural building meshes, trees, ramps, UI graphics,
textures and sounds are original procedural work created for the game, except
for separately credited assets below. No manufacturer texture is used on the
uploaded single-colour STL; its aluminium material is generated locally.

The compact freestyle personal watercraft and twin-pontoon racing inflatable
are independent stylised designs. They are not licensed manufacturer models
and do not imply an association with a watercraft or outboard manufacturer.

Dependencies retain their own licenses:

- Three.js (including Reflector, RoundedBoxGeometry and BufferGeometryUtils): MIT.
- Vite and its build dependencies: see installed package license files.
- Playwright: Apache-2.0; development and testing only.

Dependency versions and package integrity hashes are locked in package-lock.json.

Operating principles were researched from official BRP and Yamaha documents;
see RESEARCH.md for links. No manufacturer manual text, diagrams, model meshes
or photos are included. Two user-supplied still photos informed the craft proportions
and colours; they are not bundled or used as textures.

Geographic shoreline, building footprints, roads, piers, parks, beaches and bridge
alignments are derived from **© OpenStreetMap contributors**, licensed under
**ODbL 1.0**: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright . The derived database is
provided as editable `src/data/mooloolaba.json`; source URLs, hashes and rebuild
instructions are in `src/data/README.md`. The application displays this attribution.
No third-party map tiles are downloaded while playing. Original procedural facade
details, estimated heights and enlarged game bridge clearances do not establish
survey accuracy or suitability for navigation.

The updated jet-ski body is an original Blender 4.3.2 model, generated by
`art/build_sparkplug.py`, with editable `art/sparkplug.blend` and bundled
`src/assets/sparkplug.glb`. Its small baked ambient-occlusion atlas is original
and embedded in the GLB. GLTFLoader is part of Three.js (MIT). No Google Earth
images, tiles or textures are included.

The supplied spray video was used only as a visual reference. No video frames,
TikTok overlays, logos or audio are distributed. All new tinny meshes, roof and
window atlases, leaf masks and stone dressing are original procedural work.
Additional residential and breakwater geometry comes from the same OSM extracts
and remains covered by the ODbL database attribution above.

The 2026-10-19 coastal material pass uses original image-generation output for
water, panoramic clouds, sand, grass, rock and timber. These invented images are
not Mooloolaba aerial/satellite photographs and contain no copied logos or
external stock art. They are shipped locally as six WebP files; origin and
asset preparation are recorded in `src/assets/textures/README.md`.

Northern coastline extension (2026-10-19): OpenStreetMap XML retrieved from
https://www.openstreetmap.org/api/0.6/map?bbox=153.099,-26.6775,153.142,-26.653 .
Same ODbL 1.0 attribution applies. Exact source is retained compressed under
`src/data/sources`, with its SHA-256 in the editable map database. The coast,
roads and footprint plan derive from OSM; terrain and facade detail are authored.

Riptide 500 (2026-10-19): original procedural offshore racing-cat geometry,
cockpit crew and invented livery. The user-supplied Maritimo #12 photograph and
specification table were used as visual/layout references only; no photograph,
brand logo or manufacturer model is bundled. The 500 km/h setting is fictional
arcade tuning. Exact reference engine identity and performance are unverified.

Apex 300 (2026-10-19): original procedural performance-PWC geometry and invented
APEX 300 livery, using the user-supplied champagne/red/black jet-ski side photo
as a shape/colour reference only. No Sea-Doo logos, photograph pixels or licensed
manufacturer geometry are bundled. Speed, power and extreme trim are explicitly
fictional arcade settings, not claims about the reference production model.
